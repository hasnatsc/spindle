Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery is 3–5 business days across Bangladesh. Dhaka metro orders usually arrive within 2–3 days. We'll call or WhatsApp you before the rider heads out.
What does shipping cost?
Shipping is free on orders over ৳2,000. Below that, a flat charge (typically ৳80, confirmed by delivery district) is added at checkout.
How do I pay?
Cash on Delivery everywhere in Bangladesh — pay the rider when your order arrives. bKash, Nagad and card payments via SSLCommerz are coming soon.
Can I return an item?
Yes — 7-day easy returns for unused items in original packaging. Contact us with your order number and we'll arrange the pickup.
How do I track my order?
Sign in and open Order History in My Account. Each order shows its current status, payment state and shipping state.
Can I change or cancel an order?
You can cancel or change an order any time before our team confirms it — call us or use the contact form with your order number.
